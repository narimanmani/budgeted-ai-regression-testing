# Prospective v9 native-component and recorded-outcome study

This is a protocol template, not a completed experiment or a claim of TOSEM
readiness. The executable registry validator blocks native admission until the
source cases, factual contracts and complete byte closure have been registered.
The current planning frame targets two previously unused upstream settings and
two independently sourced mechanisms in each. It makes no claim that a native
component test is an independently deployed application case study.

## Questions and boundaries

The study asks whether the unchanged CG1 history/impact gates retain useful
ordering under selective and incomplete dependency evidence, whether controlled
operational judgment errors propagate through acquired history, and when the
cost of profiling and retained evidence outweighs testing more tasks. The
reference remains an executable factual contract. No human answer-quality
assessment, new model family, industrial sampling or general semantic oracle is
claimed.

Separate two sources of evidence throughout:

1. Pinned upstream production paths execute source-derived task contracts in
   both states of actual upstream bug/fix pairs. These observations establish
   what those paths do on the enrolled cases. A reversed bug fix is a controlled
   historical reintroduction, not a newly observed natural regression.
2. Counterfactual policies consume sealed recorded observations and durations.
   The factorial analysis characterizes policy behavior conditional on those
   recordings. It does not physically execute a native application for every
   policy cell. A separate fresh pass checks native outcome stability on the
   same registered cases; it is not a new application or defect sample.

If only production functions and classes can be enrolled, the output title,
report and manuscript must say *native component study*. Complete application
validation remains open until entry-point configurations and actual application
execution demonstrate the full claimed boundary. Upstream independence is
evidence about implementation authorship; it is not evidence that this study's
tasks, interventions and analysis were independently designed.

## Fixed native frame and resource account

The small planned frame is two settings, two upstream case pairs per setting,
eight public tasks per setting, two states per pair, and three separately
executed passes: **192 primary native component requests**. Every case state
executes the entire setting catalog, including unaffected controls. A fourth
fresh pass adds **64 confirmation requests**, giving **256 core requests**.
The fourth pass is registered before the first primary output and is not chosen
because a primary result looks favorable. There are four independent upstream
cases in this frame, not 192 independent defects.

The planning cap is 400 new native-path requests, including all probes, failed
attempts, qualification and confirmation. In addition to the 256 bank requests,
reserve 32 development requests and at most 88 fresh native policy-confirmation
requests below: **376 registered requests**, leaving 24 units of contingency.
It is a researcher planning choice for the newly requested work, not a numeric
allowance supplied by the user. This plan allows **zero new LLM generation
requests** and no paid service. Any need for a model call requires a separately
declared resource design before dispatch; the CPU/component path must not
silently call a provider.

The completed previous campaign stays closed at 4,938/5,000 generation-oriented
native units and 13,459.630427200318/43,200 startup-inclusive generation-allocation
seconds. Preserve its receipt and counters. New component-call counts are a
separate unit/account and must not be hidden by resetting or expanding the
closed campaign. Report cumulative generation calls unchanged when no new model
request is made; also report the separate new component-request total.

Every native request gets a reservation, immutable code/input/configuration
identity, raw result or error, monotonic elapsed duration and completion status.
Installation, source checkout, application setup, dependency extraction,
profiling and checking CPU time are recorded separately. Model, GPU, energy and
cloud-rental cost claims are outside this zero-generation protocol.

## Controlled conditions

Before any validation output, also register four actual fresh native
policy-confirmation cells: the two settings crossed with unchanged CG1 and
full retesting, on the first A fault introduction, with empty history, exact
checking and observed impact evidence. Case A is the ordinal source-based
choice, independent of policy scores. Every cell has the same 28-native-request
ceiling. CG1 physically pays three old executions for each of eight tasks
(24 requests), then attempts at most two fresh old/new pairs (four requests).
Full retesting pays no profile and executes all eight fresh pairs (16 requests),
leaving twelve units unspent under the same allowance. Actual source-based
admission can make CG1 spend fewer than its 28-unit maximum; no unused unit is
invented as consumed. Total maximum demand is 44 per setting, or 88 requests.

Seal the predecision inputs and CG1 selection before any selected new-state
response. Do not reuse the recorded bank as physical execution or permit current
reference outcomes to choose tests. Keep native/provider receipts and actual
profile, selected, feature/checker and setup costs. These cells are a small
actual native confirmation of a cold first-change decision. They do not turn
the longitudinal history/noise factorial into a live experiment or establish
full application-level external validation.

The complete replay frame crosses two impact levels, two operational checker
levels, two sequence orders and two whole-trajectory budgets. It uses each of
the three native passes as a separate recorded world, without counting those
passes as independent source defects.

- **Impact:** use dependencies exposed by the pinned production execution;
  then deterministically omit 25% of eligible observed dependency edges with a
  precommitted hash mask independent of current failure labels. This is an
  authored loss of evidence. Do not construct the initial admission set from
  known failing tasks. An empty or unverifiable set follows CG1's original
  fallback. Report realized admission sets and whether the perturbation changed
  any selection. Nonselective evidence cannot support a selective-impact claim.
- **Operational checker:** the exact frozen contract supplies the accurate
  operational result in one arm. In the other, a precommitted hash mask flips
  each observed endpoint verdict with probability 0.25, independently of its
  truth, before feedback enters a policy. The reference remains unchanged and
  inaccessible to policy selection. This is a controlled noise intervention,
  not observed real-world checker accuracy. Report realized flips, false
  positive/negative rates and their propagation through later history. The
  artificial mask changes reliability at the fixed recorded checker cost, with
  separately measured replay mask-construction CPU. It does not compare
  differently priced real checker implementations.
- **History:** all policies begin with no operational history. They acquire
  history only from their own earlier paid selected pairs. Compare registered
  recurrence-blocked and mechanism-interleaved orders, each containing sixteen
  fault/repair episodes built from the two historical pairs. These are authored
  intervention sequences and repeated faults. The labels “helpful” and
  “misleading” are findings to assess, not guaranteed treatment effects or a
  reason to inject favorable history. No complete reference or other arm's
  observations may become free history.
- **Budget:** 128 and 256 native-request units per sixteen-episode trajectory,
  charging acquisition before selected execution. Compare all methods under
  each same whole-trajectory allowance. Do not impose the previous K2/K4 pair
  ceilings and then call unrestricted full retesting budget matched.

The seven registered arms are seeded random, cost only, impact only, history
only, always combined, unchanged CG1, and budgeted retest all in the catalog
order. The random/full-retest arms need no fresh profile. Cost-based methods
pay the profile they consume. Identical selections are reported as identical
treatments, not counted as evidence for distinct mechanisms.

This produces **672 recorded-outcome trajectories and 10,752 decisions** for
the template counts. These are analytical cells derived from 192 primary
native observations. They are not 672 live native experiments or new defects.

## Cost and scheduling rules to finalize before freeze

Use the same primary native-request unit across all arms. A pair costs two
requests. The unchanged CG1 estimator consumes exactly three old-state samples
per task and exact state. Its median-derived estimate may not use new-state
durations. The common allocator, after acquisition, sets the pair ceiling to the
smaller of the catalog size and
`remaining_requests // (2 * remaining_episodes)`. All seven arms use this same
allocator. Unused requests carry forward. It must reserve acquisition and selected requests before
dispatch, use only known remaining budget and past durations, and retain
underfill, errors and overruns. A selector cannot inspect the actual cost or
result of an unselected future request to decide affordability.

Reuse a profile or observed trace only when its exact code, task input and
configuration/state digest recur. Charge its acquisition once to each
standalone policy allocation that consumes it, with no refund after a mechanism
is disabled or evidence is omitted. Do not charge all policies a common
expensive acquisition when simpler methods do not need it. Conversely, do not
lend research-bank profiles to CG1 for free. Maintain physical bank requests
separately from hypothetical per-policy allocation sums; summing all replay
allocations is not physical campaign usage.

Charge the setting's measured common setup allocation once per standalone
trajectory, equally across all arms. Bank field `common_setup_seconds` includes
only setup outside the per-query native durations, such as model startup and
resident worker imports/training. A Vanna per-query SQLite copy/connection or
other work already included in `elapsed_seconds` is not charged again as common
setup. Add exclusive native, feature and checker durations to that common setup
allocation for the reported total. Record `decision_cpu_seconds` for pure
predecision profile/impact construction, ranking, CG1 selection, input hashing
and feedback receipt construction. Record `operational_mask_cpu_seconds`
separately for operational verdict/mask handling. These are measured replay
controller CPU durations, not native execution or model inference durations.
Keep them separate from `total_allocated_wall_seconds`, which sums only the
recorded native, checker, feature and common-setup wall components. Add CPU to
that wall allocation only in the explicitly named hybrid
`total_allocated_native_wall_plus_controller_cpu_proxy_seconds`; this proxy is
not complete actual wall time. Controller wall time is not measured. The CPU
measurements and hybrid proxy can vary on reanalysis,
while selections, outcomes and allocations from frozen native recordings must
remain unchanged. These are instrumented allocation sums, not
causal production speedups. Primary budgets match requests; they do not match
wall time, GPU kernel time, idle residency, energy or cloud rental charges.

Report matched-budget detection/alarm outcomes at both levels, cost accumulated
after each episode, profile reuse, remaining budget, unique task coverage and
the point at which recurrence amortizes acquisition. Unrestricted full retesting
can additionally supply a clearly separate diagnostic. When the total allowance
is too small to run the whole suite, the budgeted retest-all arm must retain its
actual partial execution and uncovered later changes; it cannot claim complete
retesting. Allocation is spread across remaining episodes by the common formula
above; catalog order is the budgeted retest-all ordering. At the 256-unit
sixteen-episode allowance, that arm executes all eight tasks at every change.
At 128 units it executes a partial suite, which must be labeled as such.

The predeclared amortization analysis repeats the same counterfactual design
over the first four and first eight episodes, with allowances of eight or
sixteen requests per episode. The primary remains sixteen episodes and
128/256 units. These shorter-horizon replays use the same native bank and add
no physical observations or independent cases. Only the interleaved prefixes
balance both cases at all three horizons, so they provide the interpretable
amortization contrast. Blocked prefixes of four/eight episodes contain only
case A, while sixteen contains A and B; their change confounds horizon with case
composition and remains descriptive. No general production amortization
threshold is claimed.

## Analysis and claim gates

Keep true contract outcomes in an evaluator-only structure. Record predecision
inputs and ordered selections before looking up the selected native observation.
The operational adapter reveals only the selected output, its charged duration
and potentially perturbed checker verdict. Profile outputs must not be converted
into selected paired history. Verify each CG1 decision by replaying its exact
immutable inputs; enforce its original strict history schema.

Report every fixed arm, per setting/case/family, per factor cell and at both
budgets. Compare CG1 against each simpler arm as well as always-combined.
The best simpler score observed on validation is a descriptive envelope, not
a deployable selector trained on held-out truth. Report misses, true detections,
reference-discordant alarms, repair alarms, acquisition costs, selected costs,
feature/checker CPU time and the selection counts for each mechanism.

Calculate factorial differences only for contrasts actually instantiated:
impact must change admission/selection, own positive history must exist, and
checker noise must be realized. If a factor never reaches a distinct treatment,
mark its interaction unidentified. With only four upstream cases, numerical
differences characterize this finite case set; repeated masks, task copies or
requests cannot supply broad-population confidence. Any uncertainty must use
upstream cases as the independent cluster and disclose the small cluster count.

Within each setting, case order, recorded pass, impact-completeness level,
checker level and total allowance, the four fixed arms supply the registered
impact-by-history contrast:
`combined - impact_only - history_only + cost_only`. Report it for detections,
discordant alarms and allocated requests. Its difference between noisy and
exact checking is the registered judgment moderation contrast. These compare
complete sequential policy regimes; their acquired histories can differ because
earlier selections differ. They do not hold an identical externally supplied
history constant or identify general real-world checker error prevalence.

Completion means the entire registered frame and audits finish, or a retained
budget/execution failure explains the shortfall. Stop at the fixed cap, not when
a favorable difference appears. Null results and simpler-method wins are valid
outcomes. Do not add runs because precision or a positive-result threshold was
missed. Additional independent cases require a new prospective extension.

This small follow-up can fill mechanism and end-to-end cost gaps. It does not
by itself establish broad portable guidance, full application-level validation
or acceptance by TOSEM. Those claims stay open unless their own evidence gates
are satisfied.
